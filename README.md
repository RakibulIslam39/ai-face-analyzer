# 🧠 AI Face Analyzer (in-browser)

Estimates age, emotion and gender from a camera photo or an uploaded image.
Everything runs **in your browser** with [face-api.js](https://github.com/vladmandic/face-api)
(TensorFlow.js). Photos are never uploaded or stored.

The results are rough AI estimates and can be wrong.
